How long does it take to see SEO results with Keba Labs?
+

Most clients begin seeing measurable improvements in organic traffic and keyword rankings within 60-90 days. Significant, competitive rankings typically appear within 4-6 months. Our AI-powered approach accelerates the process considerably compared to traditional SEO methods. We provide monthly reports so you can track progress transparently.

Do you work with startups or only established businesses?
+

We work with businesses of all sizes — from funded startups building their digital foundation to established enterprises scaling their market presence. We have specific packages and strategies designed for early-stage companies, as well as enterprise-level growth programs for larger organizations. What is AI automation and how can it help my business?
+

AI automation involves building intelligent systems that handle repetitive business tasks automatically — such as lead follow-up emails, customer onboarding sequences, appointment scheduling, data analysis, report generation, and CRM updates. Our clients typically save 15-30 hours per week and see faster response times, better lead conversion, and reduced operational costs after implementing our AI automation systems.

SEO retainers start from ₹25,000/month, web development projects from ₹50,000, and performance marketing management from ₹20,000/month (plus ad spend). We offer customized packages for businesses with multiple needs. Contact us for a free consultation and custom proposal tailored to your specific requirements.
