Our Journey

The Keba Labs Story

From a single founder's vision to Chennai's most advanced digital growth company — this is how we got here.

2019

The Beginning

RK Milton founded Keba Labs with a clear mission — to help Chennai businesses grow through intelligent, data-driven digital strategies. Starting with just 3 clients and a bold vision, the foundation was set.

2020

Building the Team

As demand grew, we assembled a specialized team of SEO experts, web developers, and creative strategists. Our first major enterprise client validated our approach — delivering 400% organic traffic growth in 6 months.

2021

Going AI-Native

We made the decisive shift to integrate AI tools across all our services — from AI-powered content strategy to machine learning-backed keyword intelligence. This set us apart from every other agency in Chennai.

2022

100 Clients Milestone

Keba Labs crossed 100 active clients, expanded to serve businesses across Mumbai, Bangalore, and Hyderabad, and launched our proprietary AI Automation service offering.

2023

National Recognition

Featured in leading Indian business publications as one of the most innovative digital growth companies in South India. International client base grew to include Singapore and UAE-based brands.

2024

The Future We're Building

200+ projects delivered. Launching new AI-powered product lines and enterprise packages. Expanding our team to 30+ specialists. Chennai's most trusted AI-powered digital growth company — and we're just getting started.
